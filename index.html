MEET YOUR NEW CLICKING COMPANION

Small paws.
Big possibilities.

Capybara Clicker Pro is a casual incremental game. Collect coins by tapping the capybara, then spend them on upgrades that improve your earnings.

Build up your production, unlock new characters, and explore different weather settings as you progress. The appeal is simple: make a little progress, choose your next upgrade, and watch your collection grow.

EASY DOES IT

Find your capy rhythm.

  1. Start tapping. Click or tap the capybara to collect your first coins.
  2. Invest in upgrades. Use the boosters on the right to increase earnings per click or per second.
  3. Personalize your game. Check the character and weather menus for new unlocks.
  4. Keep growing. Balance active clicking with upgrades to build your coin production.

A LITTLE HELP FROM THE HERD

Curious, little capy?

Which game is featured on this page?

Play Capybara Clicker Pro, a casual idle game where you collect coins and unlock upgrades.

Do I need other players to start?

No. Capybara Clicker Pro is a single-player game, so there is no multiplayer lobby to fill.

How do I play on a phone?

Tap the capybara and the upgrade buttons. If the game feels cramped, rotate your phone or open the game in a separate tab.

Why is the game not loading?

The game loads from an external provider and needs an internet connection. Try refreshing the page or using the direct game link below the player.
